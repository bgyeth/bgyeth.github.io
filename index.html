Fast Launch Digital

Community Operations Manager

Aug 2022–May 2025

Making a 500+ member paid community easier to navigate.

Fast Launch Digital was a paid crypto and finance education community operating across Discord and Telegram. Community operations was a one-person function and I owned it: access checks and onboarding through support, moderation, documentation and retention work, while the core admin team covered the other parts of the business.

The operating workflow combined self-serve welcome material, searchable FAQs and pinned resources with manual review for payment and referral edge cases. Early member check-ins gave me lifecycle signals, and evidence-led escalation kept support and moderation decisions consistent.

The largest single build was the member education library: a step-by-step walkthrough for each exchange members actually used, and portfolio guides banded by risk level. Giving the library a resource channel of its own allowed members to find answers independently and helped reduce repeated support requests.

02

Printers Lounge

Community Operations Lead

Oct 2025–Present

Designing paid access around verification and single-use invitations.

Printers Lounge is a private paid research community on Telegram, built around a one-time Stripe payment. I built the site on Lovable, configured the Stripe checkout and payment flow, and designed the access workflow that connects the two. I supported the October 2025 launch and now lead day-to-day community operations.

The flow verifies payment before issuing a single-use invitation, then moves each member through onboarding, access review and ongoing moderation. Payment exceptions and trust decisions stay with a person rather than a rule.

03

Freelance

Web3 Risk & Due Diligence Consultant

Oct 2021–Aug 2022

Research and client education, before the community work.

Private investors brought me in to look at early-stage Web3 projects before they committed money. Each engagement ended in a written assessment: who was behind it, how the business was supposed to work, what the token did, what the roadmap promised, and where the security risk sat.

The job was really translation. Clients were putting money at risk on things they could not read, so every report had to make sense to the least technical person who would see it, and follow the same shape as the last one so two projects could be held up against each other. That habit is what the community work is built on.

02Operating approach

Operating approach

01

Make the common path self-serve.

Good onboarding and searchable answers reduce avoidable support.

02

Automate routine steps, not trust decisions.

Repeatable work can be standardised. Payment exceptions, access edge cases and trust calls stay with a person.

03

Document enough for a clean hand-off.

Clear rules and ownership turn individual effort into an operation another person can understand, review and run.
